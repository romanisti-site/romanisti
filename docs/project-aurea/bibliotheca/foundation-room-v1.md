# Foundation Room — Architectural Concept v1.0

**Project:** Project Aurea  
**Institution:** Romanisti  
**Room:** Foundation  
**Status:** Constitutionally Locked  
**Release:** RC9.2

## Constitutional Purpose

The Foundation Room preserves the permanent inheritance of Romanisti. It does not function as a browsing library or temporary exhibition space. It is the chamber in which the institution's fixed principles are encountered.

## Locked Composition

- The entrance is marked only by the word **FOUNDATION**.
- A central skylight illuminates the rear wall and the architectural reading stand.
- The North Star is centered on the rear wall:

  > A publication that sets out to be as eternal as the city itself.

- **Codex I — De Aeternitate** is displayed on the central reading stand as the first great work produced from the constitutional foundation.
- Permanent side-wall tablets house:
  - Project Aurea
  - Liber Constitutionis
  - Liber Lapidum
  - Liber Conventus
- Roman busts remain restrained historical anchors.
- An SPQR medallion is inset into the floor.
- Materials are limited to travertine, walnut, bronze, parchment, and restrained stonework.

## Prohibitions

- No subtitle or decorative symbols beneath **FOUNDATION**
- No visible book stacks
- No temporary exhibitions
- No banners or flags
- No modern monitors or display systems
- No decorative storytelling that competes with the constitutional hierarchy

## Institutional Hierarchy

1. Foundation — the room
2. Constitutional inheritance — the permanent wall tablets
3. North Star — the rear-wall declaration
4. Codex I — the first work emerging from the foundation
